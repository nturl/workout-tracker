Cardio components

Ch 10. Frequency rules that layer on top of the weekly structure.

⏱️Tabata
2 sessions / week
8 rounds × 20s all-out / 10s rest = 4 minutes per session.
Modes (randomize per session): burpees, mountain climbers, jumping jacks, squat thrusts, treadmill, rowing, KB swings, cycling.
Greenfield: "Two Tabata sessions per week, especially if you are doing everything else recommended in this chapter." Study basis: 16 min/wk Tabata yielded gains over 4×30-min steady-state.
🫁VO2 Max
bi-weekly
5 × 4-min intervals at 87-97% max HR with ~4min easy recovery between.
Modes: bike, treadmill, rower, swim, elliptical, outdoor running.
2014 study: minimum effective dose for cardiovascular maintenance is bi-weekly. Chosen cadence here.
🔥Mitochondrial Training
Tuesday bookend
4 rounds × 30-60s all-out + 4min easy active recovery.
Active recovery options: walk, mobility, foam roll, dance, anything that doesn't tax the same muscle groups.
Distinct from Tabata. Longer work intervals + longer recovery. Ch 12 prescribes as bookend to Tuesday's 7-min workout.

Tabata vs Mitochondrial: don't conflate

Tabata
  • 8 rounds × 20s on / 10s off
  • 4 min per session
  • 2 sessions / week
  • Single exercise per session, randomized across sessions
  • Source: Ch 10
Mitochondrial
  • 4 rounds × 30-60s on / 4min off
  • ~16-20 min per session
  • 1 session / week (Tue)
  • Single mode (bike/row/etc)
  • Source: Ch 12

Cadence principles

  • •Strength: 2x / week, ≥48hrs apart. Mon + Fri.
  • •Tabata: 2x / week, ≥48hrs apart. Don't stack on a strength day. App randomizes exercise mode across sessions.
  • •VO2 Max: bi-weekly (every other Thursday).
  • •Optimal timing: late afternoon / early evening, ≥3hrs before bed. Morning is acceptable when afternoons are booked.
  • •Tuesday slip rule: if Tuesday's HIIT doesn't happen, do it Thursday instead.
  • •Adventure / Sport are not strict. Sub Tuesday-equivalent if Saturday adventure runs >90 min hard.
